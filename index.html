Authority & Identity

Authority persists between streams. Your balance and rank belong to your stable Kick account ID, not only your visible username.

Usernames are stored as readable snapshots. If your display name changes, your underlying Authority account and history remain tied to the same stable identity.

Claims

Use !claim when eligible.

Claims are subject to a cooldown. Attempting to claim too early is rejected, and no Authority is added.

Valid identified claim attempts are recorded by Authority Dealer.

Betting

Betting opportunities open and close throughout the stream.

Use !bet yes <amount> or !bet no <amount>.

A wager may be rejected if betting is not currently open, you lack enough Authority, you already wagered on the current event, or the command is invalid.

Rejected wagers do not deduct Authority.

Resolution

Accepted wagers are settled only after the outcome has been resolved.

Opening, closing, resolution, winning wagers, and losing wagers are recorded by Authority Dealer.

Once resolved, the same betting event cannot be settled again.

Viewer 1v1

Viewer 1v1 redemptions enter the persistent challenger queue.

Viewer wins: +75,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

The payout is attached to the active challenge itself and is awarded automatically when the challenge is resolved.

Run It Back!

Run It Back! uses the same persistent challenger system.

Viewer wins: +250,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

Authority Dealer determines the correct prize from the active challenge. The operator does not manually choose the payout amount.

Overdelegated

When Overdelegated lands, every eligible viewer receives +100,000 Authority.

Eligibility depends on Authority Dealer identifying your Kick account during the current Authority Session.

Recognized activity can include:

  • ordinary Kick chat activity
  • Authority Dealer commands
  • betting activity
  • Channel Reward activity
  • challenger activity
To guarantee eligibility, interact with chat or Authority Dealer at least once during the current Authority Session.
Silent Viewers

Simply having the stream open does not guarantee that Kick exposes your account identity to Authority Dealer.

Completely silent viewers who never generate an identifiable interaction cannot be guaranteed an Overdelegated payout.

This prevents the system from pretending it can identify accounts the platform never actually revealed.

Ranks, Wraith & Paragon

Authority-changing events can automatically trigger progression.

Rank, Wraith, and Paragon state are evaluated by the backend after qualifying Authority changes.

You do not need to type !rank for legitimate progression to be recognized.

Channel Rewards & Challenges

Certain Channel Rewards create persistent challenger entries.

Queue state, challenge starts, resolutions, cancellations, and prize events are preserved.

Duplicate redemption protection prevents the same reward redemption from creating another legitimate challenger entry.

Corrections & Refunds

If a legitimate missed Authority payment is confirmed, Authority Dealer includes an audited correction system.

Refunds are positive-only corrections, not a general-purpose balance editor.

A correction records the target account, exact amount, reason, balance before and after, execution identity, and operator information when available.

Audit & Fairness

Authority Dealer keeps persistent records for important game activity.

  • successful and rejected claims
  • accepted and rejected wagers
  • betting lifecycle events
  • wager settlements
  • rank progression
  • challenge activity and payouts
  • Overdelegated payouts
  • refunds and corrections
The Ledger

Questions or disputes

If something appears wrong, say what happened. Where possible, the operator can compare Authority history, wagers, challenge records, payouts, and corrections against the persistent state maintained by Authority Dealer.

If a legitimate missed payment is confirmed, the exact amount can be corrected through the audited refund system instead of silently rewriting somebody’s balance.

The House rule: if Authority moves, the system should be able to explain why.
