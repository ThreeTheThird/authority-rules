THE RULES

Know the system.

Authority Dealer follows the account behind the name, records important game activity, and protects consequential actions from accidental duplication.

Authority & Identity

Authority persists between streams. Your balance and rank belong to your stable Kick account ID , not only your visible username.

Usernames are stored as readable snapshots. If your display name changes, your Authority balance and history remain tied to the same underlying account.

Claims

You can use !claim once every 20 minutes.

If you try to claim again before the 20-minute cooldown is over, Authority Dealer declines the claim and tells you how much time remains until you can claim again.

Once the cooldown has finished, you can claim again normally.

Betting

Betting opportunities open and close throughout the stream.

Use !bet yes <amount> or !bet no <amount>.

A wager may be rejected if betting is closed, you lack enough Authority, you already wagered on that event, or the command is invalid.

Rejected wagers do not deduct Authority.

Resolution

Accepted wagers settle only after the outcome has been resolved.

Opening, closing, resolution, winning wagers and losing wagers are recorded by Authority Dealer.

Once resolved, the same betting event cannot be settled again.

Unique Viewer Reward

Viewer 1v1

Viewer 1v1 is a unique Viewer Reward that places you into the persistent challenger queue for a one-on-one match against Three.

Your challenge remains in the queue until it is started, resolved, or legitimately cancelled.

Viewer wins: +75,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

Unique Viewer Reward

Run It Back!

Run It Back! is a unique Viewer Reward and the best-of-three version of Viewer 1v1.

It uses the same persistent challenger system as Viewer 1v1. The first player to win two games wins the challenge.

Viewer wins: +250,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

Ranks

Your rank reflects your position within the Authority system.

As your Authority changes, your rank can change with it. Rank progression is persistent between streams and is evaluated automatically after qualifying Authority changes.

You do not need to type !rank for progression to be recognized. That command simply shows your current rank.

Channel Rewards & Challenges

Certain Channel Rewards create persistent challenger entries.

Queue state, challenge starts, resolutions, cancellations and prize events are preserved.

Duplicate redemption protection prevents the same redemption from creating another legitimate challenger entry.

THE HOUSE CAN BE GENEROUS

Overdelegated

Want a shot at a major Authority payout? Use your Channel Points to redeem a spin on the Cursed Wheel and see what The House gives you.

If the Cursed Wheel lands on Overdelegated, every eligible viewer receives +100,000 Authority.

To receive the payout, Authority Dealer must have identified your Kick account during the current Authority Session.

Recognized participation can include:

  • sending an ordinary message in Kick chat
  • using an Authority Dealer command
  • placing a wager
  • redeeming a Channel Reward
  • participating in the challenger system
Silent viewers: simply having the stream open does not guarantee that Kick exposes your account identity to Authority Dealer. If you never generate an identifiable interaction during the session, your account cannot be guaranteed an Overdelegated payout. To make sure you are recognized, interact with chat or Authority Dealer at least once during the current Authority Session.

Audit & Fairness

Authority Dealer keeps persistent records for important game activity.

  • successful and rejected claims
  • accepted and rejected wagers
  • betting lifecycle events
  • wager settlements
  • rank progression
  • challenge events and payouts
  • Overdelegated payouts
  • refunds and corrections

Persistent Identity

The readable name attached to your account can change. The underlying identity Authority Dealer trusts does not depend on that display name alone.

This protects your Authority balance, game history, challenge records, payouts and corrections if your username changes.

THE LEDGER

Questions, disputes & missed payouts

If you believe an Authority payment, challenge reward, Overdelegated payout, refund or other legitimate reward was missed, take note of the date, approximate time, reward or event involved, and the amount you expected if you know it.

Screenshots are also helpful. With that information, the relevant Authority Dealer records can be checked to determine whether a mistake actually occurred.

Chat can move quickly, especially during active parts of the stream, so refund requests or payout concerns mentioned only in chat can sometimes be missed in the moment.

If you have a payout, refund or Authority concern:

Email the date, approximate time, reward or event, expected amount if known, and any relevant screenshots to:

threethethirdgames@gmail.com

That inbox is checked before each stream, so issues can be reviewed and addressed in a timely manner.

Privacy

Authority Dealer does not collect or store sensitive personal information.

The system stores only the platform and game information needed to operate Authority Dealer, such as your stable Kick account ID, display-name snapshots, Authority balance, rank, wagers, challenge activity, payouts and related audit records.
