Authority
Dealer Rules

Authority is the stream’s persistent viewer currency. Claim it, wager it, climb ranks, enter challenges, and compete for your place at the top.

If Authority moves, there should be a record explaining why.

Core Commands

These are the main commands viewers need to participate.

!claim Claim Authority when eligible.
!authority Check your current Authority balance.
!rank Check your current rank.
!bet yes <amount> Bet on YES while a market is open.
!bet no <amount> Bet on NO while a market is open.
!rules Returns this official rules page.

Authority & Identity

Authority persists between streams. Your balance and rank belong to your stable Kick account ID, not only your visible username.

Usernames are stored as readable snapshots. If your display name changes, your underlying Authority account and history remain tied to the same stable identity.

Claims

Use !claim when eligible.

Claims are subject to a cooldown. Attempting to claim too early is rejected, and no Authority is added.

Valid identified claim attempts are recorded by the system.

Betting

Markets open and close during the stream.

Use !bet yes <amount> or !bet no <amount>.

Bets may be rejected if the market is not open, you do not have enough Authority, you already wagered on that market, or the command is invalid.

Rejected wagers do not deduct Authority.

Market Resolution

Accepted wagers are settled only after the market is resolved.

Market opening, closing, resolution, winning wagers, and losing wagers are recorded by Authority Dealer.

Once resolved, the same market cannot be settled again.

Viewer 1v1

Viewer 1v1 redemptions enter the challenger queue.

Viewer wins: +75,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

The correct prize is attached to the active challenge and awarded automatically during resolution.

Run It Back!

Run It Back uses the same persistent challenger system.

Viewer wins: +250,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

The operator does not manually choose between the two challenge payouts.

Overdelegated

If Overdelegated lands, every eligible viewer receives +100,000 Authority.

Eligibility depends on Authority Dealer identifying your account during the current Authority Session.

  • ordinary Kick chat activity
  • Authority Dealer commands
  • betting activity
  • Channel Reward activity
  • challenger activity
To guarantee eligibility, interact with chat or Authority Dealer at least once during the current session.

Silent Viewers

Simply having the stream open does not guarantee that the platform exposes your account identity to Authority Dealer.

Completely silent viewers who never generate an identifiable interaction cannot be guaranteed an Overdelegated payout.

This rule exists so the system does not pretend it can identify viewers that Kick never exposes to it.

Ranks, Wraith & Paragon

Authority-changing events can automatically trigger progression.

Rank, Wraith, and Paragon state are evaluated by the backend after qualifying Authority changes.

You do not need to run !rank for a legitimate progression event to be recognized.

Channel Rewards & Challenges

Certain Channel Rewards create challenger entries.

Queue state, challenge start, resolution, cancellation, and prize events are stored persistently.

Duplicate redemption protection prevents the same redemption from creating another legitimate challenger entry.

Corrections & Refunds

If a legitimate missed Authority payment is confirmed, Authority Dealer includes an audited correction system.

Refunds are positive-only corrections. They are not a general-purpose balance editor.

Corrections record the target account, exact amount, reason, balance before and after, execution identity, and operator information when available.

Audit & Fairness

Authority Dealer keeps persistent records for important game activity.

  • claims and rejected claims
  • wagers and rejected wagers
  • market lifecycle events
  • wager settlements
  • rank progression
  • challenge events and payouts
  • Overdelegated payouts
  • refunds and corrections

Questions or Disputes

If something appears wrong, explain what happened in chat.

Where possible, the operator can compare Authority history, market events, wagers, challenge records, payouts, and corrections against the persistent system state.

If a legitimate missed payment is confirmed, the exact amount can be corrected through the audited refund system instead of silently rewriting a viewer’s balance.

The House rule: if Authority moves, the system should be able to explain why.
