Rules 06
01

Authority & Identity

Authority is persistent. Your balance and rank carry between streams.

Your Authority account is tied to your stable Kick account ID, not only your visible username.

Usernames are stored as readable snapshots, but the stable account ID is what protects your balance and history if your display name changes.

Same account. Across every stream.
02

Claims

Use !claim to collect Authority when eligible.

Claims are subject to a cooldown. If you claim too early, the attempt is rejected and no Authority is added.

Successful and rejected claims are recorded when the system has enough valid identity information to process the attempt.

Respect the cooldown.
03

Betting

Markets open and close throughout the stream.

Use !bet yes <amount> or !bet no <amount>.

Bets may be rejected if the market is closed, you do not have enough Authority, you already wagered on that market, or the input is invalid.

Rejected wagers do not deduct Authority.

One market. One position.
04

Market Resolution

Accepted wagers are settled only after the market is resolved.

Market opening, closing, resolution, winning wagers, and losing wagers are recorded by Authority Dealer.

Once a market has been resolved, the same market cannot be settled again.

Open. Close. Resolve.
05

Viewer 1v1

Viewer 1v1 redemptions enter the persistent challenger queue.

Viewer wins: the active challenger receives 75,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize is awarded.

The correct payout is tied to the active challenge itself and is handled automatically during resolution.

Challenge accepted.
06

Run It Back!

Run It Back! uses the same persistent challenger system.

Viewer wins: the active challenger receives 250,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize is awarded.

The operator does not manually choose between the 75,000 and 250,000 payouts.

Same system. Higher stakes.
07

Overdelegated

Overdelegated is a rare Wheel result.

When it lands, every eligible viewer receives 100,000 Authority.

Eligibility is based on Authority Dealer identifying your Kick account as participating during the current Authority Session.

  • ordinary Kick chat activity
  • Authority Dealer commands
  • betting activity
  • Channel Reward redemptions
  • challenger activity
Be seen to benefit.
08

Overdelegated Eligibility

To guarantee eligibility, interact with chat or Authority Dealer at least once during the current Authority Session.

Completely silent viewers who the platform never exposes to Authority Dealer cannot be guaranteed a payout.

Repeated activity does not create duplicate eligibility records.

Viewer identity is anchored to the stable Kick account ID.

Presence must be observable.
09

Ranks, Wraith & Paragon

Authority-changing events can automatically trigger progression.

Rank, Wraith, and Paragon state are evaluated by the backend after qualifying Authority changes.

You do not need to type !rank for Authority Dealer to recognize legitimate progression.

Paragon unlock state is persistent and protected from repeated false unlocks.

The ledger remembers progression.
10

Channel Rewards & Challenges

Certain Channel Rewards create persistent challenger entries.

Queue order, challenger state, start, resolution, cancellation, and payout records persist through the system.

Duplicate redemption protection prevents the same reward redemption from creating another legitimate challenger.

One redemption. One entry.
11

Corrections / Refunds

If a legitimate missed Authority payment is confirmed, the system includes an audited correction path.

Refunds are positive-only corrections, not a general balance editor.

A correction records the target account, exact amount, reason, balance before, balance after, execution identity, and operator information when available.

Duplicate execution protection prevents the same correction from paying twice.

Mistakes can be fixed — with a record.
12

Audit & Fairness

Authority Dealer keeps persistent records for important game activity.

  • successful and rejected claims
  • accepted and rejected wagers
  • market lifecycle events
  • wager wins and losses
  • rank / Wraith / Paragon transitions
  • Channel Reward and challenger events
  • challenge payouts
  • Overdelegated payouts
  • refunds and corrections
Every important transaction leaves a trace.
13

Questions or Disputes

If something appears wrong, explain what happened in chat.

Where possible, the operator can compare Authority history, markets, wagers, challenger records, payouts, and correction history against the persisted system state.

If a legitimate missed payment is confirmed, the exact amount can be corrected through the audited refund system instead of silently rewriting someone’s balance.

THE HOUSE RULE: if Authority moves, the system should be able to explain why.
Disputes are checked against the ledger.
