Authority Dealer Rules

Authority is the stream’s persistent viewer currency. Claim it, wager it, climb ranks, enter challenges, and survive whatever The House decides comes next.

These rules explain how Authority is earned, wagered, paid out, tracked, and corrected. The goal is simple: if Authority moves, there should be a record explaining why.

Getting Started

!claim Claim Authority when eligible.
!authority Check your current Authority balance.
!rank Check your current rank.
!bet yes <amount> Bet Authority on YES while a market is open.
!bet no <amount> Bet Authority on NO while a market is open.
!rules Opens this rules page.

Authority & Identity

Authority is persistent. Your balance and rank carry between streams.

Your Authority account is tied to your stable Kick account ID, not only your visible username. Usernames are stored as readable snapshots, but the stable account ID is what protects your balance and history if your display name changes.

This helps prevent a renamed account from being treated as a different Authority account and protects against a payout being assigned only because somebody typed a matching display name.

Claims

Use !claim to collect Authority when eligible.

Claims are subject to a cooldown. If you claim too early, the claim is rejected and no Authority is added.

Successful and rejected claims are recorded when the system has enough valid identity information to process the attempt.

Betting

When The House opens a market, viewers may wager Authority on YES or NO.

  • !bet yes <amount>
  • !bet no <amount>
A wager may be rejected if the market is not open, the market is already closed, you do not have enough Authority, you already placed a wager on that market, or the command is invalid.

A rejected wager does not deduct Authority.

Accepted wagers are recorded. Market opening, closing, and resolution are also recorded so the market lifecycle can be reconstructed later if needed.

Once a market is resolved, accepted wagers are settled and the same market cannot be paid out again.

Viewer 1v1

Viewer 1v1 redemptions enter the challenger queue.

Viewer wins: the active challenger receives 75,000 Authority.
Three wins: no Authority prize is awarded.

The Viewer Win action resolves the challenge and awards the correct prize automatically.

Run It Back!

Run It Back! uses the same persistent challenger system.

Viewer wins: the active challenger receives 250,000 Authority.
Three wins: no Authority prize is awarded.

The prize amount is attached to the active challenge itself. The operator does not manually choose between a 75,000 or 250,000 payout.

Overdelegated

Overdelegated is a rare Wheel result.

Every eligible viewer receives 100,000 Authority.

Eligibility is based on whether Authority Dealer identified your Kick account as participating during the current Authority Session.

The system can identify participation through recognized activity such as:

  • ordinary Kick chat activity;
  • Authority Dealer commands;
  • betting activity;
  • Channel Reward redemptions;
  • challenger activity.
To guarantee Overdelegated eligibility, interact with chat or Authority Dealer at least once during the current Authority Session.

Completely silent viewers who the platform never exposes to Authority Dealer cannot be guaranteed an Overdelegated payout.

Repeated activity does not create repeated eligibility entries. Viewers are identified using their stable Kick account ID.

Ranks, Wraith & Paragon

Authority-changing events can cause automatic progression.

Rank, Wraith, and Paragon state are evaluated by Authority Dealer after qualifying balance changes. You do not need to run !rank for the backend to recognize a legitimate progression event.

Paragon unlock state is persistent and protected so the same genuine unlock is not repeatedly treated as a new unlock.

Challenges & Channel Rewards

Certain Channel Rewards create challenger entries. Challenger queue order and lifecycle state are persistent.

Duplicate redemption handling is protected by the redemption identity so replaying the same redemption does not create another legitimate challenger entry.

Challenger starts, resolutions, cancellations, and prize awards are recorded so their history can be reviewed later.

Refunds & Corrections

Authority Dealer includes an audited correction system for situations where a viewer can demonstrably show that Authority should have been paid but was not.

Refunds are positive-only corrections. They are not a general-purpose balance editor.

A successful correction records information including:

  • the target viewer’s stable Kick account ID;
  • the readable username snapshot;
  • the exact Authority amount;
  • the balance before and after the correction;
  • the reason for the correction;
  • the operator identity when available;
  • a unique execution reference;
  • session context where available.

Duplicate execution protection prevents the same refund execution from being paid twice.

Corrections are designed to be traceable rather than handled through invisible manual database edits.

Fairness & Audit History

Authority Dealer keeps persistent records for important game activity.

Depending on the event, this can include:

  • successful and rejected claims;
  • accepted and rejected wagers;
  • market opening, closing, and resolution;
  • wager wins and losses;
  • rank progression events;
  • Wraith and Paragon transitions;
  • Channel Reward and challenger lifecycle events;
  • challenge prizes;
  • Overdelegated payouts;
  • administrative refunds and corrections.

Balance-changing records store the viewer identity and relevant before/after balance information so the operator can compare the recorded event against the persisted balance.

If there is a dispute, the goal is to check the system record instead of relying on memory.

Important General Rules

  • Do not assume a rejected command changed your balance.
  • Do not assume simply having the stream open guarantees Overdelegated eligibility.
  • Accepted wagers are final once the market is resolved.
  • Duplicate or replayed destructive actions are protected where the system requires it.
  • Authority balances and ranks persist between streams.
  • Stable Kick account identity is more important than a changeable display name.

Questions or Disputes

If something appears wrong, raise it calmly in chat and explain what happened.

Where possible, the operator can review Authority Dealer history, market events, wager records, challenger records, payout records, and correction history to determine what the system actually did.

If a legitimate missed payment is confirmed, the audited refund system exists specifically so the exact amount can be corrected without silently rewriting somebody’s balance.
