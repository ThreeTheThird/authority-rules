THE RULES

Know the system.

Authority Dealer follows the account behind the name, records important game activity, and protects consequential actions from accidental duplication.

Authority & Identity

Authority persists between streams. Your balance and rank belong to your stable Kick account ID , not only your visible username.

Usernames are stored as readable snapshots. Changing your display name does not create a new Authority identity.

Claims

Use !claim when eligible.

Claims have a cooldown. A claim made too early is rejected and no Authority is added.

Valid identified claim attempts are recorded by Authority Dealer.

Betting

Betting opportunities open and close throughout the stream.

Use !bet yes <amount> or !bet no <amount>.

A wager may be rejected if betting is closed, you lack enough Authority, you already wagered on that event, or the command is invalid.

Rejected wagers do not deduct Authority.

Resolution

Accepted wagers settle only after the outcome has been resolved.

Opening, closing, resolution, winning wagers and losing wagers are recorded by Authority Dealer.

Once resolved, the same betting event cannot be settled again.

Viewer 1v1

Viewer 1v1 redemptions enter the persistent challenger queue.

Viewer wins: +75,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

The correct payout belongs to the active challenge and is awarded automatically when resolved.

Run It Back!

Run It Back! uses the same persistent challenger system.

Viewer wins: +250,000 Authority.

Three wins: no Authority prize.

Authority Dealer determines the correct payout from the active challenge.

THE HOUSE CAN BE GENEROUS

Overdelegated

When Overdelegated lands, every eligible viewer receives +100,000 Authority.

Eligibility depends on Authority Dealer identifying your Kick account during the current Authority Session.

Recognized participation can include:

  • ordinary Kick chat activity
  • Authority Dealer commands
  • betting activity
  • Channel Reward activity
  • challenger activity
To guarantee eligibility, interact with chat or Authority Dealer at least once during the current session.

Silent Viewers

Simply having the stream open does not guarantee that Kick exposes your identity to Authority Dealer.

A completely silent viewer who never generates an identifiable interaction cannot be guaranteed an Overdelegated payout.

Ranks, Wraith & Paragon

Authority-changing events can automatically trigger progression.

Rank, Wraith and Paragon state are evaluated after qualifying Authority changes.

You do not need to type !rank for legitimate progression to be recognized.

Channel Rewards & Challenges

Certain Channel Rewards create persistent challenger entries.

Queue state, challenge starts, resolutions, cancellations and prize events are preserved.

Duplicate redemption protection prevents the same redemption from creating another legitimate challenger entry.

Corrections & Refunds

If a legitimate missed Authority payment is confirmed, Authority Dealer includes an audited correction system.

Refunds are positive-only corrections , not a general-purpose balance editor.

Audit & Fairness

Authority Dealer keeps persistent records for important game activity.

  • successful and rejected claims
  • accepted and rejected wagers
  • betting lifecycle events
  • wager settlements
  • rank progression
  • challenge events and payouts
  • Overdelegated payouts
  • refunds and corrections

Persistent Identity

The readable name attached to your account can change. The underlying identity Authority Dealer trusts does not depend on that display name alone.

That protects balances, history, payouts and corrections from username changes and ambiguous name matching.

THE LEDGER

Questions or disputes

If something appears wrong, explain what happened. Where possible, Authority history, wagers, challenge records, payouts and corrections can be compared against the persisted record.

If a legitimate missed payment is confirmed, the exact amount can be corrected through the audited refund system instead of silently rewriting somebody’s balance.

The House rule: if Authority moves, the system should be able to explain why.
